## Beyond Markdown: Authentic Enterprise Deliverables
Industrial plant managers, reliability heads, and regulatory auditors do not sign off on raw markdown text or chat responses. The Sovereign AI Workbench generates real, fully formatted, professional Microsoft Office and PDF deliverables:
- **Microsoft Word (.docx):** Formal executive approval notes with tables and sign-off blocks.
- **Microsoft Excel (.xlsx):** Multi-tab calculation workbooks with live, recalculable formulas.
- **Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx):** 16:9 widescreen executive briefing presentation decks.
- **Adobe PDF (.pdf):** Formal compliance audit reports with two-pass canvas page numbering.
- **Deliverable Validator:** Deep structural OpenXML ZIP and magic byte validation before user download.
