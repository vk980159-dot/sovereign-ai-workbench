# 00. Industrial Persona Use Cases Overview
**Status:** [VERIFIED]

The Sovereign AI Workbench is built for cross-functional industrial plant personnel:
- **Field & Inspection Engineers:** Digitize paper work orders, inspect photos of physical cracks.
- **Reliability Engineers:** Audit sensor trends against ISO tolerances, perform root-cause analysis.
- **Maintenance Teams:** Generate work orders and spare parts replacement requests.
- **Safety & Compliance Teams:** Audit incident reports against OSHA/industrial SOPs.
- **Executive Management:** Review structured Word approval notes and PowerPoint briefings.
- **IT & Security Administrators:** Enforce air-gap compliance and verify cryptographic ledgers.
