# 02. Problem Statement (SIH26117)
**Status:** [VERIFIED]

## Official Title
**SIH26117:** Sovereign On-Premise Agentic AI Workbench using Open-Weight Multimodal LLMs for Confidential Industrial Work.

## The Core Industry Crisis
Industrial manufacturing plants, oil refineries, nuclear installations, power grids, and aerospace facilities deal with highly classified intellectual property:
1. Equipment failure logs containing proprietary metallurgy and operational stresses.
2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and safety manuals.
3. High-resolution photographs of critical component fractures and fatigue cracks.
4. Scanned historical maintenance work orders with physical signatures.

Enterprise policies and national defense regulations strictly prohibit uploading this data to public cloud AI APIs due to data sovereignty, espionage risks, and regulatory fines (e.g. DPDP Act, GDPR, ITAR).

## The SIH26117 Mandate
Build a completely self-hosted, air-gapped system that:
- Uses exclusively open-weight models running on local hardware.
- Performs multi-step autonomous planning, execution, and verification.
- Ingests scanned PDFs, photographs, and internal manuals.
- Delivers formal engineering approval notes, spreadsheets, and briefing decks.
- Provides cryptographic mathematical proof that no data leaves the premises.
