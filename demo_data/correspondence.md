### Memo Content
During the mid-August quarterly sensor calibration on Turbine Unit 4 (TRB-702-U4), diagnostic telemetry revealed an emerging drift on Radial Bearing #3:

1. **Telemetry Observations**:
   - Baseline temperature recorded 3 months prior was 70.2°C.
   - Temperature during the August 18 peak shift drifted upward to 73.5°C (+3.3°C increase).
   - Radial vibration amplitude shifted from 2.1 mm/s to 2.8 mm/s.

2. **Analysis & Recommendations**:
   - Thermocouple TH-03 recalibration was completed and sensor drift was ruled out.
   - Lube oil flow rate through the Bearing #3 manifold remains within nominal limits (14.2 L/min).
   - Suspect minor inner-race micro-pitting or thermal boundary layer degradation.
   - Recommended that the field inspection team conduct an on-site physical and thermographic audit during the next scheduled cycle.
   - If Bearing #3 temperature surpasses the SOP-IND-702 continuous limit of 75.0°C or vibration exceeds 3.5 mm/s, immediate Level 1 remediation and formal Approval Note escalation must be triggered per plant operating guidelines.
